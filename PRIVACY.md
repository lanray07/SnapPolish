# SnapPolish Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

SnapPolish helps users turn screenshots and product visuals into polished marketing assets. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information You Provide

SnapPolish may use screenshots, images, prompts, brand preferences, captions, and other content you choose to add inside the app.

## Photos And Media

SnapPolish requests access to selected photos only so you can import screenshots or images for editing. The app does not need access to your full photo library unless you explicitly grant it through iOS.

## Voice Input

If you use voice input, SnapPolish may request microphone and speech recognition permissions so your spoken prompt can be converted into text. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings.

## Purchases And Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. SnapPolish does not receive or store your full payment card details.

## AI Features

When AI features are enabled, content you submit may be sent to the configured AI service provider to generate or improve marketing assets. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or private content that you do not want processed for this purpose.

## Data Retention

SnapPolish stores app content and preferences locally on your device unless a future feature clearly states that cloud sync or server processing is being used.
